## Minions

!!! info "Minions"
    Keep a tally of your encounters with minions. Surviving 10 encounters gives you one XP roll.

### Skeletons

| Qty | Level | Treasure | Reactions |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `d6+2` | 3 | -- | always fight to the death |

Undead.

Crushing weapons attack Skeletons at `+1`. 

Arrows are at `-1` against both skeletons and zombies.

Skeletons and zombies never test morale.

### Zombies

| Qty | Level | Treasure | Reactions |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `2d6` | 3 | -- | always fight to the death |

Undead.

Arrows are at `-1` against both skeletons and zombies.

Skeletons and zombies never test morale.

### Goblins

| Qty | Level | Treasure | Reactions |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `d6+3` | 3 | `-1` | `1` flee if outnumbered<br>`2-3` bribe (`5 gp` per goblin)<br>`4–6` fight |

Goblins have a `1 in 6` chance of gaining surprise, thus acting before the party. If they do act before the party, roll reactions.

### Hobgoblins

| Qty | Level | Treasure | Reactions |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `d6` | 4 | `+1` | `1` flee if outnumbered<br>`2–3` bribe (`10 gp` per hobgoblin)<br>`4–5` fight<br>`6` fight to the death |

### Orcs

| Qty | Level | Treasure | Reactions |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `d6+1` | 4 | Normal | `1-2` bribe (`10 gp` per orc)<br>`3–5` fight<br>`6` fight to the death |

Orcs are afraid of magic and must test morale each time one or more is killed by a spell. If a spell caused their number to drop below 50%, they will test morale at `-1`.

They never have magic items in their treasure: treat any rolled magic as `d6 x d6` gold pieces instead.

### Trolls

| Qty | Level | Treasure | Reactions |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `d3` | 5 | Normal | `1–2` fight<br>`3–6` fight to the death[^1] |

[^1]: If a dwarf is present in the party, trolls will automatically fight to the death.

Trolls regenerate, unless killed by a spell, or unless a character uses one attack to chop an already killed troll to bits. If this does not happen, roll a die for every killed troll on its next turn. On a `5 or 6`, the troll will come back to life and continue to fight.

If a dwarf is present in the party, trolls will automatically fight to the death.

### Fungi Folk

| Qty | Level | Treasure | Reactions |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `2d6` | 3 | Normal | `1-2` ask for bribe (`d6 gp` per fungus)<br>`3–6` fight |

Any character taking damage from the fungi folk must save versus level 3 poison or lose 1 life.

Halflings add their level on this save. 

## Bosses

!!! info "Bosses"
    Defeating a boss gives one XP roll.

### Mummy

| Level | Life | Attacks | Treasure | Reactions |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 5 | 4 | 2 | `+2` | always fight |

Undead.

Any character killed by a mummy becomes another mummy and must be fought by the party.

Mummies are attacked at `+2` by the Fireball spell.

Mummies never test morale.

### Orc Brute

| Level | Life | Attacks | Treasure | Reactions |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 5 | 5 | 2 | `+1` | `1` bribe (`50 gp`)<br>`2–5` fight<br>`6` fight to the death |

Orcs may not have any magic items; treat as `2d6 x d6` gold pieces instead.

### Ogre

| Level | Life | Attacks | Treasure | Reactions |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 5 | 6 | Normal | Normal | `1` bribe (`30 gp`)<br>`2–3` fight<br>`4–6` fight to the death |

Each hit from an ogre inflicts 2 life points of damage.

### Medusa

| Level | Life | Attacks | Treasure | Reactions |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 4 | 4 | Normal | `+1` | `1` bribe (`6d6 gp`)<br>`2` quest<br>`3–5` fight<br>`6` fight to the death |

All characters at the beginning of the battle must save versus a level 4 gaze attack or be turned to stone. Petrified characters are out of the game until a [Blessing spell]() is cast on them. Rogues add half their level to this save.

### Chaos Lord

| Level | Life | Attacks | Treasure | Reactions |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 6 | 4 | 3 | 2 at `+1` | `1` flee if outnumbered<br>`2` fight<br>`3–6` fight to the death |

Before the fight begins, roll `d6` to determine if the Chaos Lord has any special powers: 

| Roll | Power | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `1–3` | no powers | |
| `4` | evil eye | characters must roll `4+` or be at `-1` on all defense rolls until the chaos lord is slain |
| `5` | energy drain | any character taking a wound from the chaos lord must roll `4+` or lose `1` level |
| `6` | hellfire blast | before combat, all characters must roll `6+` or lose `2` life points;<br>Clerics add 1/2 level to this roll |

When you kill a chaos lord, roll a die; on a 5 or 6 a character of your choice finds a [Clue]().

### Small Dragon

| Level | Life | Attacks | Treasure | Reactions |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 6 | 5 | 2 | 3 at `+1` | `1` Sleeping[^2]<br>`2–3` bribe[^3]<br>`4–5` fight<br>`6` quest |

[^2]: Sleeping: all characters can attack at `+2` on their first attack

[^3]: Bribe dragon: all the gold of the party, with a minimum of `100 gold` or one magic item

On each turn of the dragon, roll `d6`:

* On a `1 or 2` the dragon breathes fire, inflicting 1 life to all characters who fail to save versus level 6 dragon breath (each character adds 1/2 level, rounded down).
* If the dragon does not breathe, he bites 2 random characters. 

Small dragons are never met as random monsters.

## Weird Monsters

!!! info "Weird Monsters"
    Weird monsters are equivalent to bosses. Defeating a weird monster gives one XP roll, with the exception of Invisible Gremlins.

### Minotaur

| Level | Life | Attacks | Treasure | Reactions |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 5 | 4 | 2 | Normal | `1-2` bribe (60 gp)<br>`3–4` fight<br>`6` fight to the death |

Due to the power of his bull-rush charge, the first Defense roll against a minotaur is at `-1`. 

Minotaurs hate halflings.

### Iron Eater

| Level | Life | Attacks | Treasure | Reactions |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 3 | 4 | 3 | -- | `1` flee<br>`2-3` bribe (`d6 gp` to distract[^4])<br>`4-6` fight |

[^4]: Iron Eater cannot be fooled with fools gold

Defense rolls against the iron eater do not enjoy bonus from heavy armor (shield and light armor count). 

If the monster hits, the character takes no damage but loses his armor, shield, main weapon, or `3d6 gp`, in this order.

### Chimera

| Level | Life | Attacks | Treasure | Reactions |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 5 | 6 | 3 | Normal | `1` bribe (`50 gp`)<br>`2–6` fight |

On every of the chimera’s turns, roll `d6`. On a 1 or 2 the chimera breathes fire instead of performing its multiple attacks. All characters must save versus level 4 fire or lose 1 life.

### Catoblepas

| Level | Life | Attacks | Treasure | Reactions |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 4 | 4 | Normal | `+1` | `1` flee<br>`2-6` fight |

All characters at the beginning of the battle must save versus a level 4 gaze attack or lose 1 life.

### Giant Spider

| Level | Life | Attacks | Treasure | Reactions |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 5 | 3 | 2 | 2 rolls | always fight |

Characters taking a wound must save versus level 3 poison or lose an additional life. 

Due to the spider’s webbing, the party may not withdraw from this fight unless they cast a [Fireball spell]() to burn the webs.

### Invisible Gremlins

A band of gremlins steal `d6+3` objects from the party. You must surrender objects from any of your characters in this order of preference

1. magic items
2. scrolls
3. potions
4. weapons
5. gems
6. coins (in bundles of `10 gp` each)

If the gremlins steal ALL of your equipment, they will leave a thank you message that counts as a [clue]().

The gremlins have no combat stats because it is impossible to fight them. Encountering them gives no XP roll.
